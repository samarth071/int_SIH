## Project Overview
We are building [SANJEEVANI MESH], [Unified Disaster Management Platform with a React Native mobile app for citizens and a web-based admin dashboard for authorities. 
Three-Phase Approach:
Before: Location & disaster-specific risk mitigation, preparedness and safety guidelines.
During: Emergency communication and resource coordination using LoRa-based communication when conventional networks fail.
After: Centralized coordination of relief resources such as food, shelter, medical aid and financial assistance through NGOs, volunteers and organizations. 
Detailed Explanation:
Citizens can receive preventive alerts and guidelines, report emergencies even in low/no-connectivity conditions, while authorities can monitor incidents, prioritize requests and allocate resources. 
How it Addresses the Problem:
Reduces communication gaps during disasters, enables last-mile emergency reporting, improves situational awareness, and ensures better coordination of relief and recovery efforts. 
Innovation & Uniqueness:
Offline/low-connectivity emergency communication using LoRa + location-aware disaster preparedness + real-time resource coordination in a single end-to-end platform covering Before → During → After disaster management.
